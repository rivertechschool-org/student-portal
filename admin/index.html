The admin dashboard. None of the cards carries a waiting count.

Nothing tells you there is a queue. Parent link requests, enrolment applications, materials requests and room bookings all wait quietly — no card shows a number, and there is no badge in the nav. Turning on the matching email notifications in 👤 Profile is how you find out without remembering to look.

Part 1 · People

Getting a family from an application to a working account is three separate steps, and the portal deliberately does not join them up.

Enrolment

📋 Enrollment. Six counters across the top — Families, Students, Pending, Approved, Denied, Waitlisted — then a filter bar (status, school year, a search that matches student name, parent name or application number) and Export CSV.

The queue is grouped by family, and children are hidden until you click. A family row reads "Whitfield Family — 2 student(s)" with a ▶ marker. Click it to open the children underneath. A family with more than one child also gets Approve All and Deny All on the header row.

View opens the application in four tabs — Student, Family, Medical and Review. Review is where the admin notes, the buttons and the parent's typed signature live.

Approving

Two buttons, and the difference is whether the pupil can be signed in yet:

Approve (Active)

The pupil is enrolled and ready to be given a sign-in.

Approve (Inactive)

The record is created, but the pupil is not yet active — they can still be given a Games PIN and graded.

Either way the confirmation says exactly what it does:

"This creates their school record, medical details, waivers and the parent profile. Sign-ins are opened afterwards from the roster — nothing is emailed to the family yet."

Afterwards you get a Still to do list, and it is the most useful thing on the screen — "Milo needs their account opened", "Dana needs a parent login before they can be linked". Those two jobs are the next section.

Approve All does the same thing for every child in the family, and reports the same way — it names them in the confirmation, emails the family, and gives you one combined Still-to-do list. It approves as active; to approve a child as inactive, use their own button instead, which the confirmation reminds you of.

Deny asks for a reason in a prompt and emails it. Waitlist and Mark Under Review ask nothing at all and act immediately.

Opening accounts

Approving creates records. It creates no logins. Opening a login is a separate, deliberate act, and it is the same modal wherever you start from.

The modal tells you which of three situations you are in:

  • Address on record — the usual case. Check it and send.
  • No usable address yet — the pupil signs in by PIN and the address on file is an internal placeholder with no inbox. Type the one they actually read.
  • No address on record — nothing can be sent. Add one first.
After activating a parent from Staff & Parents, reload the screen. The row you just activated will still say "No sign-in yet" — the screen refreshes the student list rather than the parent list. The activation worked; the display did not catch up.

Staff & parents

👥 Staff & Parents. Four tables — Admins, Active Teachers, Former Teachers, Parents — plus ✉️ Invite Teacher at the top. Pupils are deliberately not here; every per-pupil action lives on the student hub instead.

Inviting a teacher takes an email address and, optionally, a name. Leave the name blank and the account is created as New Teacher with a machine-generated display name, so fill it in. ✉️ Reinvite on a teacher's row sends a fresh set-password link and kills any earlier one.

On a parent's row: ➕ Link Child if they have a login, ✉️ Open account if they do not, and their linked children as removable pills.

A parent must have a login before they can be linked to anyone. That is why a parent with no sign-in is offered only ✉️ Open account. If you try it the other way round the portal tells you so.

The orphan sign-ins card

If it appears, read it. ⚠️ Sign-ins with no account lists addresses that can sign in and land on nothing, and they cannot fix it themselves — they already have a login, so registering again is refused.

Do not guess who they are. If you know whose an address is, open that person's account from the roster using that address — it moves the sign-in onto their record. If you do not recognise it, ask before doing anything.

Parent link requests

👪 Parent Link Requests. This is the queue a parent lands in when they do not have a link code. Each request shows who is asking, the name they typed, and either a list of possible matches or "No name match found."

Clicking a candidate asks you to confirm first, naming the parent and the child and saying what they will be able to see. A ⚠️ beside a name means that pupil is already linked to somebody, and the confirmation repeats that warning — two parents on one child is ordinary, but so is a mis-click on the wrong row of a list of siblings.

The faster route for a family is a link code, which you can read out over the phone. It is on every row of Student Records under Parent Code — click to copy. Reissuing one stops the old code working but keeps everyone already linked.

Deactivating and deleting

Almost always, deactivating is the right answer.

PersonReversible optionWhat it keeps
Teacher or adminDeactivateEverything. They stop signing in; classes and history stay. Reactivate restores it.
Student, account⊘ Deactivate AccountThe record entirely.
Student, enrolmentMark as PastGrades and attendance. Removes them from live rosters and teacher views, and issues a Games PIN if they had none.
Parent— none —The only irreversible option on a parent row is Delete.

Delete asks three times: a full list of what goes, then a warning, then a prompt where you type DELETE in capitals. It removes the profile, the login and every record attached — grades, attendance, notes, medical information, contacts and parent links — and unsets the teacher on any class they taught. You cannot delete yourself; your own row says "You" instead.

Part 2 · The roster

Student records

📁 Students. Search by name or email, filter by Active / Past / All and by grade. Then ➕ Add Student, Import from CSV, 👥 Find duplicates and Manage Groups.

Eight columns, and three of them are the ones you came for:

Example only — invented names and codes.

  • Games PIN — shown in plain text on purpose, because pupils forget and staff have to read it back. ↻ issues a new one and kills the old one.
  • Pickup — the family's pickup number. A blank here means the child cannot be called at pickup, so the gap is the point.
  • Parent Code — click to copy. Reissuing keeps existing links.

Mark as Past asks for a date left and an optional reason, then tells you exactly what moved — classes archived, attending days cleared, activities ended, grades and attendance kept.

Emergency contacts live under 📁 Records. Each has a Can Pickup flag, and it is ticked by default on a new contact — untick it deliberately for anyone who may not collect the child. Marking a contact Primary clears the flag from the others automatically.

Merging duplicates

👥 Find duplicates on Student Records. This exists because staff pre-add a pupil and the pupil later signs up themselves — the records end up on one row and the login on the other.

Two ways in:

  • Merge any two profiles — for the ones an exact-name match cannot spot: Ari and Arian, Sam and Samuel, a misspelling. Pick Keep this one and Remove this one.
  • Exact-name groups — anywhere two profiles share a name outright.

Either way you get a live preview and then a review step. Read the warnings; they are the whole value of the tool:

"The profile being removed has more records than the one being kept. Check that is what you mean."
"These have different dates of birth on file — they may be two different children."
"… is left over and stops working. If that is the one they use, keep the other profile instead."

The review step separates things to decide (both profiles hold a value and they disagree — you choose), settled without asking (only one has a value) and records that just combine. Then it asks once more, and says plainly that it cannot be undone.

Merge two at a time. A group of three will tell you to.

Strikes

⚠️ Student Strikes. Maximum three per pupil, and they decay after three months. Anyone at three appears in a red card at the top under 🚨 Students at Maximum Strikes (Review Required).

+ Strike opens a modal that shows current → after and escalates its wording: first strike, second strike, then "This will be their THIRD strike and they will be flagged for review." A reason is required, and parents are emailed.

Decay is worked out when someone looks, not on a schedule. The clock restarts from the most recent strike — one strike clears three months after the latest one, two after six, three after nine. Because it is computed on reading, the count on this roster can be higher than the count inside a pupil's record until you open them. The record is the one to trust.

Part 3 · The school

Quarters

⚙️ Settings → 📅 Grading Periods → 📅 Manage Quarters. One quarter is current; nothing works it out from today's date, so it has to be set by hand.

Setting a new current quarter snapshots the old one first, and refuses to continue if that fails. You would see "Could not save grades for the outgoing quarter, so the switch was cancelled. Nothing has changed." That is the system protecting a term's grades, not an error to work around.

The current quarter decides which assignments teachers see, what the due-date picker will accept, and which grades the gradebook is showing. If a teacher says their assignments have vanished, check this first.

The bell schedule

🔔 Bell Schedule — the time blocks that periods and breaks hang off. Classes show their times from here, so a class in a period with no block reads "no time set" to everybody.

Blocks can be reordered with the ↑ and ↓ buttons or sorted by time, and the screen warns about overlaps and out-of-order blocks. Staff duties can be tied to a block, and then they follow the bell — change the time here and the duty moves with it.

Classes

🏫 Classes — every class in the school, not just yours. Filter by teacher, and tick to include past teachers when you are looking at history. A class shows both its teachers where there are two.

Changing who teaches a class is done from here. Only staff with a working sign-in can be assigned — anyone without one is listed but not selectable, because there would be nothing to attach the class to.

Attendance and reports

📋 Attendance Reports is the school-wide view: search and filter records by date, pupil and status, and take the register from the same screen with 📋 Take Attendance.

📊 Grade Reports is by quarter and class, and is where report cards and transcripts are generated in bulk.

Report cards and transcripts open in a new tab and must be opened from the portal. They carry their data across in the browser rather than in the address, so bookmarking one shows a blank page, and so does refreshing the tab. Print or save before you reload. Print / Save PDF in the corner is the only way to keep a copy.

Handle with care

The short list of things that either cannot be undone or do not warn you properly. Worth reading once before you need it.

ActionWhy it's on this list
Approving a parent link request It asks first and names both people, but it cannot be undone from that screen. Read the ⚠️ marker.
➕ Link Child from a parent's row Clicking a pupil in the list links them straight away — there is no confirm step.
Reopening a class Re-enrols everyone, including pupils withdrawn before it was closed.
Deleting anyone Three confirmations, then everything attached to them goes. Deactivate or Mark as Past instead, nearly always.
Merging profiles Cannot be undone. Read the date-of-birth and record-count warnings.
Waitlist / Mark Under Review Act immediately with no confirmation at all.
Regenerating a Games PIN The old one stops working straight away. Reissuing a parent code is the gentler one — existing links survive.
And one that is not dangerous, just confusing. A teacher who types an admin address into the bar gets a blank screen or an error rather than a polite refusal. Nothing has broken and nothing has leaked — the screen simply cannot fetch anything. If a teacher reports it, that is the answer.
