The admin dashboard. None of the cards carries a waiting count.

Nothing tells you there is a queue. Parent link requests, enrolment applications, materials requests and room bookings all wait quietly — no card shows a number, and there is no badge in the nav. Turning on the matching email notifications in 👤 Profile is how you find out without remembering to look. Days that need a sub are the same, and have no email at all — see Sub cover.

Part 1 · People

Getting a family from an application to a working account is three separate steps, and the portal deliberately does not join them up.

Enrolment

📋 Enrollment. Six counters across the top — Families, Students, Pending, Approved, Denied, Waitlisted — then a filter bar (status, school year, a search that matches student name, parent name or application number) and Export CSV.

The queue is grouped by family, and children are hidden until you click. A family row reads "Whitfield Family — 2 student(s)" with a ▶ marker. Click it to open the children underneath. A family with more than one child also gets Approve All and Deny All on the header row.

View opens the application in four tabs — Student, Family, Medical and Review. Review is where the admin notes, the buttons and the parent's typed signature live.

Approving

Two buttons, and the difference is whether the pupil can be signed in yet:

Approve (Active)

The pupil is enrolled and ready to be given a sign-in.

Approve (Inactive)

The record is created, but the pupil is not yet active — they can still be given a Games PIN and graded.

Either way the confirmation says exactly what it does:

"This creates their school record, medical details, waivers and the parent profile, and emails the family to say the place is confirmed. Their sign-in is opened afterwards from the roster — no password link goes out yet."

Afterwards you get a Still to do list, and it is the most useful thing on the screen — "Milo needs their account opened", "Dana needs a parent login before they can be linked". Those two jobs are the next section.

Approve All does the same thing for every child in the family, and reports the same way — it names them in the confirmation, emails the family, and gives you one combined Still-to-do list. It approves as active; to approve a child as inactive, use their own button instead, which the confirmation reminds you of.

Deny asks for a reason in a prompt and emails it. Waitlist and Mark Under Review ask nothing at all and act immediately.

Opening accounts

Approving creates records. It creates no logins. Opening a login is a separate, deliberate act, and it is the same modal wherever you start from.

The modal tells you which of three situations you are in:

  • Address on record — the usual case. Check it and send.
  • No usable address yet — the pupil signs in by PIN and the address on file is an internal placeholder with no inbox. Type the one they actually read.
  • No address on record — nothing can be sent. Add one first.

Staff & parents

👥 Staff & Parents. Four tables — Admins, Active Teachers, Former Teachers, Parents — plus ✉️ Invite Teacher at the top. Pupils are deliberately not here; every per-pupil action lives on the student hub instead.

Inviting a teacher takes an email address and, optionally, a name. Leave the name blank and the account is created as New Teacher with a machine-generated display name, so fill it in. ✉️ Reinvite on a teacher's row sends a fresh set-password link and kills any earlier one.

On a parent's row: ➕ Link Child if they have a login, ✉️ Open account if they do not, and their linked children as removable pills.

A parent must have a login before they can be linked to anyone. That is why a parent with no sign-in is offered only ✉️ Open account. If you try it the other way round the portal tells you so.

The orphan sign-ins card

If it appears, read it. ⚠️ Sign-ins with no account lists addresses that can sign in and land on nothing, and they cannot fix it themselves — they already have a login, so registering again is refused.

Do not guess who they are. If you know whose an address is, open that person's account from the roster using that address — it moves the sign-in onto their record. If you do not recognise it, ask before doing anything.

Parent link requests

👪 Parent Link Requests. This is the queue a parent lands in when they do not have a link code. Each request shows who is asking, the name they typed, and either a list of possible matches or "No name match found."

Clicking a candidate asks you to confirm first, naming the parent and the child and saying what they will be able to see. A ⚠️ beside a name means that pupil is already linked to somebody, and the confirmation repeats that warning — two parents on one child is ordinary, but so is a mis-click on the wrong row of a list of siblings.

The faster route for a family is a link code, which you can read out over the phone. It is on every row of Student Records under Parent Code — click to copy. Reissuing one stops the old code working but keeps everyone already linked.

Deactivating and deleting

Almost always, deactivating is the right answer.

PersonReversible optionWhat it keeps
Teacher or adminDeactivateEverything. They stop signing in; classes and history stay. Reactivate restores it.
Student, account⊘ Deactivate AccountThe record entirely.
Student, enrolmentMark as PastGrades and attendance. Removes them from live rosters and teacher views, and issues a Games PIN if they had none.
Parent— none —The only irreversible option on a parent row is Delete.

Delete asks twice: a full list of what goes, then a prompt where you type DELETE in capitals. Every delete that cannot be undone — a person, an enrolment, a whole class, an assignment with work handed in — uses that same two-step gate. It removes the profile, the login and every record attached — grades, attendance, notes, medical information, contacts and parent links — and unsets the teacher on any class they taught. You cannot delete yourself; your own row says "You" instead.

When someone cannot sign in

A pupil or parent who says they are "locked out" usually has one of these in front of them, and the first two do not mean their account is broken. Ask what it says before reissuing anything.

What they seeWhat it means
🔌 Couldn't load your account, with Try again They are signed in and the account is fine — it just did not load that time. Try again reloads it. If a line under it says the computer's clock is off, fix that first (next row).
A red bar across the top: ⏰ This computer's clock is … ahead (or behind) The device's date, time or time zone is more than five minutes out, and that makes signing in keep failing. The bar says how far out and how to fix it. On Windows: Settings → Time & language → Date & time → turn on Set time automatically and Set time zone automatically, then Sync now. Close the browser, open it again, and sign in.
⚠️ Profile Incomplete The sign-in exists but there is no record behind it. Look for them on the orphan sign-ins card under Staff & parents.
A clock an hour out is the common one. It is usually a laptop set to the wrong time zone with the time typed in by hand, so it looks right to whoever is holding it. Resetting the password or regenerating a PIN does nothing for it.

Part 2 · The roster

Student records

📁 Students. Search by name or email, filter by Active / Past / All and by grade. Then ➕ Add Student, Import from CSV, 👥 Find duplicates and Manage Groups.

Eight columns, and three of them are the ones you came for:

  • Games PIN — shown in plain text on purpose, because pupils forget and staff have to read it back. ↻ issues a new one and kills the old one.
  • Pickup — the family's pickup number. A blank here means the child cannot be called at pickup, so the gap is the point.
  • Parent Code — click to copy. Reissuing keeps existing links.

Mark as Past asks for a date left and an optional reason, then tells you exactly what moved — classes archived, attending days cleared, activities ended, grades and attendance kept.

The student hub

Open on any row. A full-screen view with the pupil's GPA and quarters in the header and twelve tabs. It is the only "view one student" screen in the portal — teachers reach the same hub from 👥 My Students → 📋 View Details, so what you see is what they see, less the Account tab.

Emergency contacts live under 📁 Records. Each has a Can Pickup flag, and it is ticked by default on a new contact — untick it deliberately for anyone who may not collect the child. Marking a contact Primary clears the flag from the others automatically.

Merging duplicates

👥 Find duplicates on Student Records. This exists because staff pre-add a pupil and the pupil later signs up themselves — the records end up on one row and the login on the other.

Two ways in:

  • Merge any two profiles — for the ones an exact-name match cannot spot: Ari and Arian, Sam and Samuel, a misspelling. Pick Keep this one and Remove this one.
  • Exact-name groups — anywhere two profiles share a name outright.

Either way you get a live preview and then a review step. Read the warnings; they are the whole value of the tool:

"The profile being removed has more records than the one being kept. Check that is what you mean."
"These have different dates of birth on file — they may be two different children."
"… is left over and stops working. If that is the one they use, keep the other profile instead."

The review step separates things to decide (both profiles hold a value and they disagree — you choose), settled without asking (only one has a value) and records that just combine. Then it asks once more, and says plainly that it cannot be undone.

Merge two at a time. A group of three will tell you to.

Strikes

⚠️ Student Strikes. Maximum three per pupil, and they decay after three months. Anyone at three appears in a red card at the top under 🚨 Students at Maximum Strikes (Review Required).

+ Strike opens a modal that shows current → after and escalates its wording: first strike, second strike, then "This will be their THIRD strike and they will be flagged for review." A reason is required, and parents are emailed.

Decay is worked out when someone looks, not on a schedule. The clock restarts from the most recent strike — one strike clears three months after the latest one, two after six, three after nine. The roster and the pupil's record work it out the same way, so the two counts always agree.

Part 3 · The school

Quarters

⚙️ Settings → 📅 Grading Periods → 📅 Manage Quarters. One quarter is current; nothing works it out from today's date, so it has to be set by hand.

Setting a new current quarter snapshots the old one first, and refuses to continue if that fails. You would see "Could not save grades for the outgoing quarter, so the switch was cancelled. Nothing has changed." That is the system protecting a term's grades, not an error to work around.

The current quarter decides which assignments teachers see, what the due-date picker will accept, and which grades the gradebook is showing. If a teacher says their assignments have vanished, check this first.

Subjects and grade bands

⚙️ Settings → Subjects & Grade Bands → Edit lists. This is the school's own vocabulary, and it is editable — you do not need anyone to change code to add a subject.

ColumnWhat it does
SubjectThe name on the class form and the report card.
Drive groupWhich top-level Drive folder a class's work files under. Core, Tech, Arts, Life, Health — and Other, for the timetable blocks that are not a taught subject: study hall, props, cover.
Other namesAliases, comma separated. A class already typed in with an older spelling resolves to this row without being renamed.
Renaming a subject carries every class with it. That is the point of editing here rather than in the database: the classes and the list move together or not at all. A subject still in use by a class cannot be deleted — you are told no rather than left with classes pointing at nothing.
The Drive folder keeps its old name. A folder is remembered by its id, not its name, so a rename here does not rename it in Drive. If the folder name matters to anyone, rename it there too.

The second tab does the same for grade bands — the bands classes are assigned to, with the grades each one covers. Adding a band is how you split Highschool or add a Kindergarten band without anyone touching code.

The bell schedule

🔔 Bell Schedule — the time blocks that periods and breaks hang off. Classes show their times from here, so a class in a period with no block reads "no time set" to everybody.

Blocks can be reordered with the ↑ and ↓ buttons or sorted by time, and the screen warns about overlaps and out-of-order blocks. Staff duties can be tied to a block, and then they follow the bell — change the time here and the duty moves with it.

Rename, on the Every day tab, gives a block a clearer name — Period 5 (Lunch) — up to 40 characters. It renames the block on every day at once and changes only the label, never the times. Teachers see that name on the rows of their week view.

Pickup, and who is late

⚙️ Settings → 🚗 Pickup. Dismissal has always carried a real timestamp — the board shows the time each child was called, to the minute, and it keeps the first time if somebody taps twice. What this adds is the line that decides which of those times count as late.

SettingWhat it does
End of dayA pickup after this is late. School time, not the browser’s. Default 2:45 pm.
Hold the email forThe first late child starts the clock. Everyone else collected late inside that window joins the same email. Default 60 minutes.
Send it toWhere the report goes. Default learn@rivertech.me.
Email about late pickupsOff means nothing is sent. Times are still recorded either way, so you lose the email and not the record.

The email arrives once, naming each child, their number, the time they were collected and how many minutes past the cutoff that was.

Changing the time takes effect at the next pickup. The cutoff is read at the moment a child is ticked, not when the board was opened, so nobody has to reload anything.
A mistaken tick no longer costs a family a late email. Undo on the board — on Called today, on Here today, or by unticking on Dismissal — takes the child off the email that is being held, and if that leaves it empty the email is not sent at all. The toast says which happened.

The board itself is 🚗 Pickup, on the Attendance screen and on every teacher's home screen. Its search runs as you type, and a family card shows only the children today's register has in, with a line to show the rest. You also get a Without a number tab, and New family number, that teachers do not.

Pickup notes

Every child on the board has a note button — ＋ when there is none, 📝 when there is one — on both Dismissal and Here today. A note is for the person at the door: "Aunt Kate is collecting today — silver estate." Staff only; parents never see it. Each note is set for:

  • Today only or Tomorrow only;
  • Pick dates — a from and a to;
  • Every day, until removed — a standing note, the usual arrangement.

Dated notes stop showing by themselves once their last day has passed, so nobody has to tidy them. The note window lists every note on the child — in force today, scheduled, or finished (greyed) — with who added it, so you can see that tomorrow is already covered before writing it twice. Any member of staff can add one; as an administrator you can edit or remove any of them.

Under each child the notes in force today read Usually, Today or Until a date. An early pickup reported for today shows there too, on both tabs — Early pickup 1:45 pm, the reason, and whether it came from a parent or from staff (see late arrivals and early pickups).

The orange edge means something is different today. A child with a dated note or an early pickup today gets an orange border, on both tabs. A standing note does not, because it is the usual arrangement.

Children who are not called at pickup

Some walk, some drive themselves, some leave with a sibling off-site. Until now the only way to say so was to leave their family number blank — and the board reads a blank number as a job not yet done, so the children who needed no chasing were exactly the ones it kept flagging.

On a pupil’s record, beside their pickup number, there is now Not called at pickup. It asks how they leave, and that reason shows on the record afterwards. An exempt child:

  • stops counting towards Without a number, so that figure is only ever real gaps;
  • is never named in a late email, because they cannot be late for something they are not called to;
  • can still be dismissed on the board as normal — the record is unaffected.
A whole household can be marked instead of one child. Where the family is marked, the per-child button disappears, because a toggle that cannot win is worse than no toggle.

Emergencies

🚨 Emergency is on the Attendance screen and every staff home screen. Any member of staff can start one, deliberately — whoever smells smoke should not have to find the office first. It puts one shared roll call on every staff screen, and pulls anyone already in the portal onto it within about twenty seconds. The teacher guide walks through the board.

The count to watch is unaccounted for. Children due in today with no register entry are counted separately as unknown and never as accounted for, because nobody knows whether they are in the building — which is also why taking the register every morning matters.

Only an administrator sees Turn off. It shows how many are still unaccounted for and unknown, then asks "Which was this?" — It was a test, Real emergency — all clear, or Keep it running, with an optional note. There is no default; pick deliberately, because that answer is saved with it, and it decides whether the entry can ever be deleted. Every staff screen then shows a green SAFE screen that each person taps away, so nobody is left wondering whether it ended or their page broke. The screen itself never says "drill", because the same roll call runs for a real emergency.

📜 Emergency log — on the Attendance screen, and on the Emergency screen when nothing is running — keeps every one: the kind, Test or Real emergency, start and end times and how long it ran, who started and ended it, and the state it ended in (how many were expected, safe, off-site, and still missing). Each entry also says in words how it was recorded — "Recorded as a test — turned off with ‘It was a test’", or "Recorded as a real emergency". It flags any that ended with children unaccounted for, and shows how long it took to account for everyone, with the average across tests. Roll call & notes on an entry shows every tick in order with the time since it started, lists exactly who was still open at the end, and takes notes afterwards. The log and each roll call download as CSV.

Tests can be cleared out of the log: 🗑 Delete test on one entry, or 🗑 Delete all … tests at the top for every one at once. Each asks first, and the entry's roll call goes with it. A real emergency, and one still running, has no delete button — those stay in the record.

Classes

🏫 Classes — every class in the school, not just yours. Filter by teacher, and tick to include past teachers when you are looking at history. A class shows both its teachers where there are two.

Changing who teaches a class is done from here. Only staff with a working sign-in can be assigned — anyone without one is listed but not selectable, because there would be nothing to attach the class to.

Sub cover

When a teacher will be away, they (or you) press 🙋 Sub Days on the class, pick a from and a to date, and leave notes for the sub. Only the days that class meets are added. You can also flag days yourself from the board below — pick the class under Flag days for a class… and press Open.

🙋 Sub Cover on the dashboard lists every flagged day from today on: the date, the class, its teacher and the notes for the sub, with a picker on each that reads Needs a sub until you choose someone. Any teacher or admin can be picked. The heading counts how many still need one, and ✕ takes a day off the list. The last two weeks fold away underneath as the record of who covered what.

Nobody is notified — not you, and not the sub. Flagged days wait on the board until someone opens it, and choosing a sub sends nothing either. Look at the board each week, and tell the sub yourself.

On the day, and only that day, the class appears under Covering today at the top of the sub's Classes page: the register, the assignments (read only) and the teacher's notes, plus a box for notes back to the teacher, which cannot be edited once added. The teacher sees who covered and what they wrote under Sub Days, and so do you on the board.

Attendance and reports

📋 Attendance Reports is the school-wide view: search and filter records by date, pupil and status, and take the register from the same screen with 📋 Take Attendance.

One student's absences

Pick a single Student in the filters and a card appears above the results: 🗓️ … : all absences — every day in the date range they were not marked present (absent, late, left early), whatever the status and type filters say, with the classes they missed that day. The line under the heading counts the full days absent and how many are excused.

Each day can be Excused with an optional reason, or Unexcused, and Excuse all does every unexcused day at once, keeping any reasons typed in the rows. Excusing is by the day: a day where they were in school but missed a class says so, and has nothing to excuse.

Late arrivals and early pickups

⏰ Late Arrivals & Early Pickups, on the Attendance screen, is the list of the ones the office already knows about. + Add takes the student, late or early, the day and the time. Riven takes them too: "Alex is being picked up at 1:45 Thursday", "Alex is arriving late at 9:05 today". A second time for the same day replaces the first.

Parents can report one themselves, beside Report an Absence. Theirs land in the same list marked reported by a parent, and you get a notification, as do the teachers of that child's classes that day.

It is a plan, never a mark. Nothing is marked on its own. Each class register that day flags the student, going by the bell schedule — "Expected to arrive late, about 9:05", "Expected to leave early, at 1:45", or "expected to miss this class" — and the daily register shows a line under the name. An early pickup for today also shows on the Pickup board. The teacher still takes the register.

📊 Grade Reports is by quarter and class, and is where report cards and transcripts are generated in bulk.

There is no D+ or D-. The school scale gives a plain D for 60–69%, below that an F, and A+ to C- above it as before. Report cards, transcripts, the gradebook and the grade dropdowns all use it. A grade recorded as D+ or D- before the change still reads correctly.
Report cards and transcripts open in a new tab and must be opened from the portal. They carry their data across in the browser rather than in the address, so bookmarking one shows a blank page, and so does refreshing the tab. Print or save before you reload. Print / Save PDF in the corner is the only way to keep a copy.

Handle with care

The short list of things that either cannot be undone or do not warn you properly. Worth reading once before you need it.

ActionWhy it's on this list
Approving a parent link request It asks first and names both people, but it cannot be undone from that screen. Read the ⚠️ marker.
➕ Link Child from a parent's row Clicking a pupil in the list links them straight away — there is no confirm step.
Deleting anyone, or a class Two steps — the list of what goes, then type DELETE — and then everything attached goes. Deactivate, Mark as Past or Close Class instead, nearly always.
Turning off an emergency Ends it for every member of staff at once. Look at the unaccounted number it shows you first.
Deleting tests from the emergency log The roll call goes with it, and it cannot be brought back. Only entries recorded as a test can be deleted — another reason to answer "Which was this?" carefully.
Assigning a sub Saved the moment you choose, and tells nobody. The sub learns of it only from their Classes page on the day, or from you.
Merging profiles Cannot be undone. Read the date-of-birth and record-count warnings.
Waitlist / Mark Under Review Act immediately with no confirmation at all.
Regenerating a Games PIN The old one stops working straight away. Reissuing a parent code is the gentler one — existing links survive.
And one that is not dangerous, just confusing. A teacher who types an admin address into the bar gets a blank screen or an error rather than a polite refusal. Nothing has broken and nothing has leaked — the screen simply cannot fetch anything. If a teacher reports it, that is the answer.
