How the portal works

Four guides, one for each kind of person

The portal shows you a different set of screens depending on who you are, which is why there is a guide each rather than one long one. Pick yours.

Parents & guardians

Parent guide

Open an account, link your child, then grades, attendance, report cards, messaging teachers and reporting an absence.
Students

Student guide

Signing in, your classes, handing work in, the games and skill trees, RTC and the store.
Teachers

Teacher guide

Your invitation and first sign-in, classes, assignments, the register, grading, and Riven.
Administrators

Admin guide

Enrolment, opening accounts, the roster, the bell schedule, attendance, reports and school settings.
Not sure which is yours? Sign in and look at the bar across the top. If it says Children's Classes you are a parent; if it has Games and Skills you are a student; if it has My Students you are a teacher; and if it has Admin you are an administrator.
