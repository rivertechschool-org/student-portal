Combat Guide

Each turn, choose a mode and build math equations to fight. Switch modes freely — Attack to deal damage, Defend to block hits, or Utility to heal and draw cards.

⚔ Attack Mode

Build a math equation from your hand cards, then evaluate it. The result becomes your damage. Select a target enemy from the dropdown before evaluating.

  • Division (÷) makes attacks hit all enemies (AoE).
  • Combo Bonus: Using more operators in one equation gives bonus damage: 1 op = +25%, 2 ops = +50%, 3+ ops = +100%.

🛡 Defend Mode

Your equation result becomes Block, which absorbs incoming damage. Block resets at the start of your next turn, so use it the turn before enemies attack.

⚡ Utility Mode

Utility lets you draw cards or heal instead of fighting. The result of your equation determines the effect:

  • Result ≤ 5 — Draw extra cards into your hand. Great for restocking when your hand is low.
  • Result > 5 — Heal HP equal to result ÷ 3 (rounded down). Use this to recover between tough fights.

When to use Utility: Switch to Utility when enemies are defending (no incoming damage) or when you need cards/HP more than damage output.

✨ Special Number Bonuses

When your equation uses at least one operator, special results grant bonus effects:

  • Perfect Squares (4, 9, 16, 25...): In Attack mode, applies 2 Exposed to the target. In Defend mode, 50% of your block carries over to next turn.
  • Prime Numbers (2, 3, 5, 7, 11...): In Attack mode, draw an extra card. In Defend mode, gain 50% more block.
  • Multiples of 10 (10, 20, 30...): In Defend mode, removes 1 debuff. In Utility mode, bonus healing equal to result ÷ 10.

💣 Enemy Intents

Enemies show their next action above their heads each turn:

  • ⚔️ Attack — Will deal damage to you. Build Block to absorb it!
  • 🛡️ Defend — Gains Block that you must break through.
  • 💪 Buff — Gains Strength, increasing all future attack damage.
  • 💀 Debuff — Applies a status effect to you.
  • 💥 Special — A powerful attack, often with debuffs attached.
  • 🔀 Split — The enemy divides into two weaker copies.

😈 Status Effects (Debuffs)

  • Confused 😵 — Your damage is reduced by 25%.
  • Exposed 🎯 — You take 50% more damage from attacks.
  • Distracted 💭 — Your Block gains are reduced by 25%.
  • Slowed 🐌 — You draw 1 fewer card each turn.

Debuffs decay by 1 each turn and expire when they reach 0.

🃏 Card Types

  • Numbers — The building blocks of equations (0–25).
  • Operators (+, -, ×, ÷) — Combine numbers. More operators = bigger combo bonus.
  • Functions (x², √, !) — Transform equation results for big damage.
  • Parentheses ( ) — Group parts of your equation to control order of operations.
  • Curses — Bad cards that clog your hand. Remove them at shops when you can.

💡 Tips

  • You can evaluate multiple equations per turn — play cards, evaluate, then play more!
  • Watch enemy intents closely. If they show a big attack, switch to Defend mode.
  • Multiplication and parentheses unlock bigger combo damage.
  • At rest sites, choose between healing 30% HP or upgrading a card.
  • Upgraded cards generate bonus temporary cards when played.
  • Relics provide passive bonuses that last the entire run.
