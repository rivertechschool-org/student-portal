# Coin and bill images

Public-domain images of US money, as works of the US government, from Wikimedia Commons.
They were cropped to the coin, given a transparent background and resized to 200 px
(the bill to 320 px wide).

The nickel is the 2004 Jefferson design. The current (2006 on) nickel's photo on
Wikipedia is used under fair use, not public domain, so it is not used here.
The bill is shown one-sided and far below actual size, as US rules on pictures of
currency require.
