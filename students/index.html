Part 1 · Getting in

There are two ways to sign in, and which one you use depends on whether you have an email address yet.

Your account

You don't sign yourself up. A parent or a teacher opens your account for you. When they do, you get an email with a link in it. Click the link, choose a password, and that's it — from then on you sign in at rivertech.me with your email address and that password.

Nothing on your record is lost. The school already has a record for you with your classes, grades and skills on it. Opening your account puts a sign-in on that record — it doesn't start you from scratch.

Two things that trip people up:

  • The link works once, and it stops working after a while. If it says the link is no longer valid, ask for a new one — you haven't broken anything.
  • Your email address has to be your own. You can't share one with your parent, because one address can only be one account.

Choose a password of at least 8 characters. Some screens will let you get away with 6, but others insist on 8, so 8 saves you doing it twice.

The Games PIN

Don't have an email account yet? You still get to play. A teacher can give you a 4-character PIN — four letters or numbers, like K7RM.

On the sign-in page, click the 🎮 Games PIN tab instead of the Email tab, type your PIN, and press Sign in with PIN.

A PIN gets you Games and Skills, and nothing else. No classes, no assignments, no messages. If you click something else the page just takes you back to Games without explaining why — that's the PIN, not a fault. For the rest you need a full account, which a parent or teacher opens.

Your PIN isn't shown anywhere in the portal. If you forget it, ask a teacher — they can read it out or give you a new one. A new one stops the old one working.

Part 2 · Your school work

Where your classes are, and how to hand work in so it actually arrives.

Your classes

📚 Classes shows every class you are in. Each card has the subject, the grade range, your teacher's name and when it meets.

Not in a class you should be in? Go to the 📊 Portal screen and press ➕ Join Class, then type the class code your teacher gives you — something like ABC123. If it doesn't work you'll be told the code is invalid; check it with your teacher rather than guessing.

Open a class and you get four buttons:

📝 Assignments

Everything set in this class, and whether you've handed it in.

📊 My Grades

Your marks for this class.

💬 Discussions

Class conversations. You can start one as well as reply.

📚 Class Resources

Material your teacher has shared — notes, links, files.

A class that's finished shows 🔒 This class is closed. You can still read your grades and history in it; you just can't add anything new.

Handing work in

Open the assignment. You can hand in typed text, a file, or both — and a pasted link (a Google Drive share link, say) counts as text.

Example only.

You have to press Submit twice. The first press turns the button into Confirm Submit? and gives you about three seconds to press it again. If you press once, see the button change, and walk away — your work is not handed in. Press it the second time and wait for it to finish.
  • Past the due date? You'll see a warning that it's overdue. You can still hand it in — it may just be marked late.
  • Changed your mind? Open it again and press Edit Submission. Your new file replaces the old one, so your teacher only ever sees one.
  • Says "Offline Assignment"? Then there's nothing to upload. It's done in class and your teacher enters the mark directly.
  • Says "Excused"? Your teacher has excused you. It won't count against you.

Grades and feedback

Once it's marked, opening the assignment shows Your Grade — a letter and the points — and, underneath, Teacher Feedback if your teacher wrote any. Read that part. It's the bit that tells you what to do differently.

Your quarter grade is built from two things: Academic (your assignments) and Participation (set by your teacher). How much each one counts depends on the class, and the class page tells you which it is — Even 50/50, Skill-Heavy 60/40 or Participation-Heavy 40/60.

Tests

Under 🧭 More → 📝 Testing. It opens with My Tests and three numbers: Pending Tests, Completed, and your Average Score.

Press Start Test. You get one question at a time with a progress bar, and Submit Test only appears on the last question. Multiple-choice questions are marked straight away; if the test has any written answers the whole thing waits for your teacher, and you'll see "Awaiting teacher review".

The minutes shown on a test are a guide, not a countdown. Nothing submits your test for you when the time is up. Keep an eye on the clock yourself, and don't leave it half-finished expecting it to send.

Messaging a teacher

💬 Messages. Pick who you want from the To: list, type, and press ✉️ Send Message.

The list has your teachers and the office on it — nobody else. The heading on that screen says "teachers, students, and parents", which is the same heading staff see. For you it is the teachers of your own classes and the school's administrators. You can't message other students here.

Skill trees

🌟 Skills is where you see what you've unlocked. Nine subjects: Math, Science, Language, Technology, Creative, Social Studies, Bible, Life Skills and Physical. Pick one and your tree for it opens.

You don't earn anything on the Skills screen itself — it's a map, not a game. Skills are unlocked by playing. A skill moves from activated to mastered as your score climbs.

Skills can go backwards. Math Dojo's Retention Gauntlet re-tests things you learned a while ago. Pass and the skill is refreshed. Fail and a mastered skill drops back to activated. That's deliberate — it's checking you still know it, not that you knew it once.

RTC — River Tech Currency

You earn RTC for real work: mastering skills, assignments, and awards your teachers give out. Your balance is on your Home screen, and on the Games and Skills screens if you sign in with a PIN.

One rate you can see for yourself: the Retention Gauntlet in Math Dojo pays 3 RTC for a recent skill, 2 for a touch-on, 1 for a retention — and each individual skill only pays once every 24 hours, so grinding the same one doesn't work.

🏦 The Bank

You can leave RTC in the bank and it earns interest each month. Taking it out costs tax — unless you withdraw during the tax-free window, which the bank screen tells you the dates of. Watch for "Tax-Free Window Active!".

🛍️ The Store

Two tabs.

Privileges

Things you can do, bought for a set number of days. Each one shows how long it lasts and what it costs, and your active ones show the time left.

Cosmetics

👤 Avatars, 🏷️ Titles and 🎖️ Badges. Buy one, then Equip it. Badges have three slots — you can wear three at once.

If a button says Need 40 more RTC, that's the price minus what you have.

Real-world things are rung up by a teacher. There's no checkout for physical items in your portal. You ask a teacher, and they take the RTC off your balance at their end.

Clubs, sport and band

🏆 Activities under 🧭 More. Two tabs — My Activities and Browse All. Each one shows the coach, where and when it meets, and how many places are taken.

The button tells you where you stand:

  • Join — you're in.
  • Tryout — a sport that picks its team; this asks to try out.
  • Full — no places left.
  • Leave — you're already in it.

🎵 Worship / Band is its own page. If you're not on the team yet you'll see 🙋 Ask to join Worship / Band — pick the instruments you'd like to play (you can pick more than one), add a note if you want, and press Send my request. You can cancel it while it's waiting, and if you're turned down you're welcome to ask again.

Part 4 · Your settings

All on the 👤 Profile screen.

What you can change

  • Your password — type a new one twice and press Update Password.
  • Your email address — you have to click a link in both your old inbox and your new one before it switches over.
  • One email setting — New Assignments, which is on unless you turn it off.
  • How the site looks — six themes, including a Light one if dark is hard to read. You can also set your own colours and a background image.
  • Your cosmetics — the avatar, title and badges you bought with RTC.

Your theme follows your account, so it looks the same on a school computer and at home.

What you can't change

Your name is read-only, and that's on purpose: it's what every register, gradebook and pickup list calls you, so it's the school's to change, not yours. If it's wrong or spelled badly, tell the office and they'll fix it.

Also set by the school and not by you: your grade level, your Games PIN, your RTC balance, and whether your account is switched on.

If a screen doesn't exist for you, it just isn't there. You won't get a "you don't have permission" message — the buttons simply aren't drawn. So if a guide or a friend mentions something you can't find, it's probably a teacher's screen.
