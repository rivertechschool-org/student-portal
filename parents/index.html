Before you start

Two things are worth knowing up front, because they explain nearly every question the office gets asked.

A parent account and a child link are two separate things

Making an account gives you a sign-in. It does not connect you to a pupil — that is a second step, and it always goes through a person at the school. This is deliberate: it is what stops anyone who can fill in a form from attaching themselves to somebody else's child.

So a brand new account looks empty, and that is normal. It fills up the moment the link is made.

There is one address to remember

rivertech.me is where you sign in, and everything else is reached from there. Bookmark it. If a link in an email ever drops you somewhere unexpected, going back to that address will sort it out.

Part 1 · Getting an account

From nothing at all to signed in and looking at your child's grades. Five steps, and two of them may already be done for you.

Which route is yours?

The school set you up

If your family has been through enrolment, the office may already have created your parent record and emailed you a link to set a password. Check your inbox before making a second account — skip to step 4.

You are opening it yourself

Perfectly normal, and the usual route. Start at step 2. You will need an email address you can read today, because a confirmation link is sent to it.

One address, one account. Your email address is your sign-in name. You cannot use the same address for your own account and for your child's — the portal will refuse it, and it is worth knowing before you pick one.

  1. New to the school? Apply first

    If your child is not enrolled yet, start at the application form rather than the sign-in page: rivertech.me/enrollment. No account is needed to fill it in.

    It has five steps:

    1. Students — names, grade, and your family address.
    2. Parents — Parent/Guardian 1 is required; a second is optional.
    3. Emergency — contacts, and who is allowed to collect your child.
    4. Medical — doctor, allergies, and anything staff need to know.
    5. Review — the terms, the waivers, and your typed signature.

    When you submit, you get an application number for each child. Save it. The admissions team reviews the application and replies to the email address you gave for Parent/Guardian 1.

    Submitting an application does not create an account. Nothing is emailed to you automatically at that point, and there is nothing to sign in to yet. Your account comes later — either from the office once the application is approved, or by making one yourself in the next step.

    Already enrolled? Go straight on.

  3. Confirm your email

    Open the email and click the link in it. It takes you to a short page that says "✅ Email Confirmed!" and then sends you back to the portal, already signed in.

    • The link works once, and it expires. Do not forward it.
    • Open it on the device you want to use, not on somebody else's.
    • If nothing arrives within a few minutes, check the spam folder before trying again.
    If the link has expired, don't register again. A second attempt with the same address will be refused. Email learn@rivertech.me and ask the office to open your account — that takes a moment at their end.

    The fast way — a link code

    Ask the office for your child's link code. It is a short code, roughly like ABCD2345, and staff can read it out over the phone or at the desk. Expand "Have a link code from the school?", type it in, and press Link with code. The connection is made immediately and your dashboard reloads with your child on it.

    • You only ever type it once.
    • One code per child — ask for each child's separately.
    • If the school reissues a code, anyone already linked stays linked.

    The other way — send a request

    No code? Type your child's full name, optionally their grade and your relationship to them, and press Send Request. A member of staff checks it by hand and you are emailed when it is done.

    The box does not suggest names as you type, on purpose. A search box here would tell anybody with an account whether a given child attends the school. So type the name in full and carefully — spelling it the way the school has it makes the match quicker.

    Got more than one child? Repeat this from ➕ Link Another Child. There is no limit.

  6. Open your child's own account (if they need one)

    Once you are linked, your child's card may show "No sign-in yet". That means the school holds their record — their classes, grades and attendance are all on it — but they have no way to sign in themselves.

    You can fix that. Press ✉️ Open their account, give an email address your child can actually open, tick the confirmation box and press Send the link. They get an email that lets them set their own password.

Part 2 · Using the portal

What is on each screen, and which one to open for the thing you actually want.

The portal has two halves

You sign in at rivertech.me and land on your dashboard. Most of the day-to-day detail lives one step further in, at 📊 Portal, which is the classes-and-messaging side. The bar across the top moves you between them.

rivertech.me/portal/
🏠 Home 📊 Portal 📚 Children's Classes 💬 Messages 👤 Profile
Five items. That is the whole of your navigation.

A parent's nav bar. Teachers and pupils see different items — if a guide or a teacher mentions a screen you cannot find, that is usually why.

ItemWhat is there
🏠 HomeYour dashboard: linked children, a calendar, and a quick look at recent work.
📊 PortalThe main parent screen — children, totals, and every quick action.
📚 Children's ClassesEvery class your children are in, and what is set in each.
💬 MessagesConversations with your children's teachers and the office.
👤 ProfileYour settings — password, email, notifications, theme. See Part 3.

Two more sections sit under a 🧭 More card at the foot of the Portal screen rather than in the bar: 📄 Documents and 🏆 Activities.

Beside the nav bar there is a 🔔 bell. It collects New Assignment Posted and Assignment Graded notices for your children, and new messages. It has Mark all read and Clear all.

Strike alerts do not appear on the bell. If your child receives a disciplinary strike you are told by email, and the current count is always on their card. Do not rely on the bell for that one.

One thing that catches people out: the heading at the top of the Portal screen reads "📚 Welcome to Classes & Messaging — access your classes, assignments…", and the classes list is headed "📚 Classes". That wording is shared with pupils and teachers. They are your children's classes.

How the GPA is put together

The four quarters are not weighted equally. Q1 counts for 20%, Q2 and Q3 for 25% each, and Q4 for 30% — so the year leans towards how it finishes. A quarter showing — has not been graded yet and is not dragging anything down.

Report card and transcript

Two documents, and they answer different questions.

📄 Report card

This year, by quarter — the marks for each class and the comments that go with them. Reached from 📄 Report Card inside your child's progress panel.

📋 Transcript

The whole school record, year by year, with credits and cumulative GPA. Reached from 📋 Transcript in the same panel, or from 📋 View Transcript in Quick Actions.

Both open in a new tab, laid out for paper, with a single Print / Save PDF button in the corner. That button is the way to keep a copy — press it and choose your printer, or choose "Save as PDF" as the destination.

Three things about those two pages. They open in a new tab, so if nothing happens, check whether your browser blocked a pop-up. They must be opened from inside the portal — bookmarking one and returning to it later shows a blank page. And refreshing the tab blanks it too, so print or save before you reload. Going back and pressing the button again always works.

With more than one child linked, 📋 View Transcript in Quick Actions asks which child first. The report card has no equivalent shortcut — open each child's progress panel and use the button there.

Classes and assignments

📚 Children's Classes lists every class your children are in. With more than one child, each card carries a 👦 line naming which of them is in it.

Open a class and a blue banner confirms where you are — "👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Parent View · Viewing your child's class" — with three buttons under Class Actions:

📝 Assignments

Everything set in this class, with a line per child: Not Submitted, Submitted – Pending Grade, Graded with the mark, Excused, Offline Assignment, or Missing.

📊 Children's Grades

A table per child — assignment, due date, status and mark out of the total. A Quarter dropdown at the top starts on the current quarter; switch it to All Time to see the whole year.

📝 Children's Notes

What the teacher has written about your child in this class — positive, neutral or negative, with the category, the note itself, who wrote it and when. Often the most useful screen on the site.

Only published work appears. Teachers draft assignments before releasing them. If your child mentions something you cannot see, it has probably not been published yet — and the class list shows the current quarter, so to look further back use 📊 Children's Grades and switch the quarter, or read the report card.

Everything here is read-only. Class discussions are for pupils and teachers, and are not shown to parents.

Activities, documents, and re-enrolling

🏆 Children's Activities

Sports and clubs, listed per child. Each shows a Sport or Club chip, the coach, where it meets, its schedule, whether it runs for a season or all year, and your child's standing — active, tryout or waitlisted. It is a read-only view; joining and leaving go through the coach or the office.

📄 School Documents

Handbooks, policies and guides for families, grouped under those headings, each with the date it was last updated. The River Tech Handbook opens the live Google Doc in a new tab — the rest open in the page, with a ← Back to Documents button.

📋 Re-Enroll for Next Year

When it is time to enrol for the next school year, this opens the application form again with your family's details already filled in. Check what has changed, correct it, and submit. It is an application, so the office processes it — the live records are not edited directly.

This is also how you correct your address, phone number or emergency contacts. There is no settings screen for those, on purpose — see what only the office can change.

The calendar on your Home screen

🏠 Home carries a month calendar built from your child's timetable — class meetings, due dates for published assignments, tests, quarter boundaries and school events. The filter pills above it (Assignments, Tests, Classes, Quarters, School Events) turn each kind on and off, and clicking a day lists everything on it. Holidays and closures are shaded.

Above the calendar sits a 🎓 Days of School Left card — how much of the year is done, with weekends and holidays already taken out.

With two or more children linked, a Select Child dropdown appears above it. Changing it redraws the calendar for that child straight away, on whichever month you were looking at.

Two switches, both on unless you turn them off, and they are the only email settings you have:

Both start ticked, because both are on for a new account. Untick what you don't want, press Save Preferences, and it takes effect on the next email.

What you see is what you get. The boxes show what the school will actually send you, including before you have ever saved anything — so opening this card and pressing Save Preferences without touching a box changes nothing at all.

Some emails are not on this list and cannot be switched off from here — a disciplinary strike, for instance, is always sent. If the volume of email is not working for you, say so to the office rather than hunting for a setting that does not exist.

Your password and your email address

🔐 Changing your password

Type the new one twice and press Update Password. It applies immediately. Forgotten it instead? Sign out and use Forgot Password? on the sign-in screen — that link is good for one hour.

✉️ Changing your email address

This is the address you sign in with, so it is deliberately careful. Type the new address twice and press Send Confirmation Link.

You must click a link in both inboxes. One email goes to your current address and one to the new address, and the change only completes when both are clicked. Until then, keep signing in with the old one. A pending change is shown in amber on that card, and submitting a different address replaces it.

How the site looks

🎨 Site Theme changes the whole portal for your account, and follows you to any device you sign in on. Six presets:

rivertech.me/portal/#profile
🎨 Site Theme
Choose a theme for the entire site — navigation, cards, backgrounds, and more.
Default Dark Midnight Blue Light Warm Ember Nord AMOLED Black

Picking one saves it immediately. Light is the one to choose if the dark theme is hard to read.

Underneath are three optional extras, each folded away until you open it:

  • Customize Colors — set individual colours yourself.
  • Background Image — a picture behind the portal, with a slider for how strongly it shows through. It needs a direct image link; Google Drive and Dropbox links will not work.
  • Button Style Override — recolour the buttons independently of the theme.

There is no separate text-size control; use your browser's own zoom (Ctrl and +, or ⌘ and +).

Signing out, and staying signed in

🚪 Logout sits in ⚙️ Account Actions on the Profile screen; the main site also has a Logout button in its header. Otherwise you stay signed in on that browser — there is no timeout and nothing to tick to stay signed in.

On a shared or public computer, sign out when you finish. Your account can see your children's grades, attendance, and medical and contact details.

🗑️ Delete Account is on the same card. It asks twice, and it cannot be undone. If what you actually want is to stop the emails, change your notification settings instead; if you want to leave the school, talk to the office first.

And a few things that simply are not there

  • A history of your child's attendance. You can report an absence and see what is already on the list, but not browse past days. Ask the office for a record.
  • Fees, invoices or payments. The portal handles none of it.
  • Permission slips. The waivers are signed once, as part of enrolment and re-enrolment; there is no per-trip form.
  • Class discussions, the school currency, games and skills. Those belong to pupils. The testing centre, the staff assistant and every admin screen belong to staff.
