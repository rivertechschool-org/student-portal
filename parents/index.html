1 · Setting up

You need an email address you can open today. That's all.

New to River Tech? Apply first at rivertech.me/enrollment. Applying does not make an account. Once you've applied, come back here and make one.
The school may have set you up already. If you got an email from River Tech asking you to set a password, use that link instead, then skip to Link your child.

  1. Create your account

    1. Go to rivertech.me.
    2. Press Create a Parent Account (under the Login button).
    3. Fill in the form and press Register.

    An example. The name and email are made up.

    • Use your own name, not your child's.
    • Your password needs at least 8 characters.
    • There's no box for your child here. You link them in step 3.
    • Each email address can only have one account, so don't use the same address you plan to give your child.

  2. Confirm your email

    1. Open the email from River Tech.
    2. Click the link in it.
    3. You'll see "✅ Email Confirmed!" and then land back in the portal, signed in.
    No email after a few minutes? Check your spam folder. Still nothing, or the link says it has expired? Don't register again (it won't let you). Email learn@rivertech.me and the office will open the account for you.

    Next time, just sign in at rivertech.me with your email and password. Bookmark that address. It's the only one you need.

  3. Link your child

    When you first sign in, your home screen looks like this. That's expected:

    rivertech.me
    👶 No Children Linked
    No children are linked to your account yet.
    ➕ Link a Child

    A new account. One button, and it's the right one.

    Press ➕ Link a Child. There are two ways to do it.

    ⚡ With a link code (fastest)

    1. Ask the office for your child's link code. It looks like ABCD2345.
    2. Press "Have a link code from the school?" at the bottom.
    3. Type the code and press Link with code.

    Your child appears right away.

    ✉️ Without a code

    1. Type your child's full name, spelled the way the school has it.
    2. Add their grade if you like.
    3. Press Send Request.

    Someone at the school checks it and emails you when it's done.

    First and last name
    e.g. 9
    Mother, Father, Guardian
    Cancel
    Send Request
    ▸ Have a link code from the school?
    ABCD2345
    Link with code

    Both ways are in the same window. The code is at the bottom.

    • More than one child? Do this once for each child, using ➕ Link Another Child. Each child has their own code.
    • The name box won't suggest names as you type. That's on purpose, to keep students' names private.

  4. Give your child their own sign-in (optional)

    If your child's card says they have no sign-in yet, the school has their record but they can't sign in themselves. To fix that:

    1. On your home screen, press ✉️ Open (your child's name)'s account on their card.
    2. Type an email address your child can open. It can't be your own.
    3. Tick the box to confirm it's right, and press Send the link.

    Your child gets an email to set their own password. Everything the school already has for them (classes, grades, skills) is waiting when they sign in.

    Younger children without an email can still play the learning games with a 4-character Games PIN from their teacher.

2 · Everyday use

Everything you can see and do once your child is linked.

Finding your way around

After you sign in you land on 🏠 Home. Most things live one click away, under 📊 Portal. The bar across the top always takes you between them:

On 📊 Portal, each child shows a badge like 0/3. That's their behavior strikes: green is none, amber is one or two, red is three. Strikes clear on their own over time, and you're always emailed when one is given.

Reporting an absence

No phone call needed. On 📊 Portal, press 📅 Report an Absence:

  1. Pick your child.
  2. Pick the first and last day they'll be away. One day? Pick the same day twice. Weekends in between are skipped for you.
  3. Add a reason if you like (for example "unwell").
  4. Press Tell the school.

The school is told straight away, and you'll see how many staff were notified. Already reported at the top of the window shows absences already on file, so you won't send the same one twice.

Late arrival or early pickup

For a dentist visit in the morning or a pickup before the end of the day. On 📊 Portal, press ⏰ Late Arrival / Early Pickup:

  1. Pick your child.
  2. Choose Arriving late or Picking up early.
  3. Pick the day (today or later) and the time.
  4. Add a reason if you like, and press Tell the school.

Your child's teachers and the office are told straight away. Plans changed? Press Withdraw next to it under Already reported.

Messaging a teacher

  1. Press 💬 Messages in the top bar.
  2. Under To:, pick the teacher (or the office).
  3. Type your message and press ✉️ Send Message.

Replies show under 📬 Recent Messages, and the 🔔 bell lights up when one arrives. The list shows your children's teachers and the school office. A teacher appears once your child is in one of their classes.

Classes, grades and teacher notes

Press 📚 Children's Classes, then open a class. You'll see three buttons:

ButtonWhat it shows
📝 AssignmentsEverything set in the class and where your child stands on each.
📊 Children's GradesA grade table. Change Quarter to All Time to see the whole year.
📝 Children's NotesWhat the teacher has written about your child. Often the most useful screen.

If your child mentions work you can't see, the teacher may not have posted it yet.

Report card and transcript

  1. On 📊 Portal, press your child's name.
  2. Press 📄 Report Card (this year, by quarter) or 📋 Transcript (every year, with credits).
  3. It opens in a new tab. Press Print / Save PDF to print or keep a copy.
Nothing opened? Your browser may have blocked a pop-up. Allow pop-ups for rivertech.me and press the button again. Print or save before you refresh that tab, or it goes blank.

Calendar, activities, documents and re-enrolling

  • Calendar (on 🏠 Home): classes, due dates, tests and school events. Press a day to see what's on it. With two or more children, pick whose calendar with Select Child.
  • 🏆 Activities: your children's sports and clubs, with the coach and schedule.
  • 📄 Documents: the handbook and school policies. Activities and Documents are under 🧭 More at the bottom of 📊 Portal.
  • 📋 Re-Enroll for Next Year (on 📊 Portal): opens the form with your family's details already filled in. It's also how you update your address, phone or emergency contacts.

3 · Your settings

All on 👤 Profile in the top bar.

To…Use this card

Change your password🔐 Change Password: type it twice, press Update Password.
Change your sign-in email✉️ Change Email: type it twice, press Send Confirmation Link, then click the link sent to both the old and new address.

What only the office can change

Some details are kept by the school on purpose, because pickup lists and emergencies depend on them. Email learn@rivertech.me (or re-enroll) to change:

  • Your name, phone number or address
  • Emergency contacts, and who can pick up your child
  • Medical information
  • Your child's grade level or classes

Grades, attendance and strikes are changed by the teacher who gave them. The portal doesn't handle fees or payments.
