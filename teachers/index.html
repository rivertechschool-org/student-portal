Part 1 · Your account

Teachers do not sign themselves up. An administrator invites you, and the invitation is the only way in — so the first five minutes are worth reading rather than guessing at.

  1. The invitation lands in your inbox

    An administrator opens 👥 Staff & Parents in the portal and presses ✉️ Invite Teacher. They type your email address and, optionally, your name. You get an email with a link.

    That link works once, and it expires. Three things burn it: clicking it twice, waiting too long, and — the one nobody expects — an email security scanner that quietly opens every link in a message before you ever see it. If you land on "⚠️ This link is no longer valid", nothing is wrong with you or your account. Ask the office to send a fresh one.

    Open it on the computer you actually intend to work on, and do not forward it. The sign-in it creates lives in whichever browser opened it.

  2. Set a password

    The link confirms your address — "✅ Email Confirmed!" — and then sends you straight to a box headed Complete Your Registration.

    rivertech.me/index.html?setup_password=true
    Complete Your Registration✕
    Welcome, Rosa!
    Your account has been created. Please set a password to complete your registration.
    Enter your password (min 8 characters)
    Confirm your password
    Set Password & Continue

    Example only — the name here is invented.

    At least 8 characters. Every screen that can ask you to set a password asks for the same thing — this one, Change Password, and the reset link — so you only decide once.

    Press Set Password & Continue and you land in the portal, signed in. From then on you sign in at rivertech.me with that address and password like anyone else.

  3. Fix your name before you do anything else

    This is the one piece of housekeeping worth doing on day one, because the portal has given you a placeholder and pupils will see it.

    Go to 👤 Profile. Under 📋 Personal Information you will find something teachers get and nobody else does: your name is editable.

    rivertech.me/portal/#profile

    Alder

    This is the name on your classes, messages and assignments. It has to be different from everyone else's.
    Save name

    Example only. That machine-shaped value in What students see is what the invitation generated — change it to Mrs. Alder, or whatever your pupils call you, and press Save name.

    • It must be different from everyone else's. If it is taken you get "… is already taken by someone else. Try another."
    • It cannot be blank — "Students need something to call you".
    • Success looks like a small "Name updated" message.
    If the administrator left the name fields blank when inviting you, your account is currently called New Teacher and the welcome box greeted you as "Welcome, New!". Same fix, same screen.

  4. What your first day looks like

    Empty, and that is correct — there is no setup wizard and nothing is wrong. Your portal home has three cards and every number on it reads zero until you build a class.

If the link doesn't work

What you seeWhat it means, and what to do
⚠️ This link is no longer valid Used, expired, or opened by a mail scanner before you got to it. Ask the office to press ✉️ Reinvite.
Your invitation link has expired. Same answer. Nothing is lost.
Told you have an account, but no email ever arrived Your address probably already had a half-finished sign-in behind it, so no new mail went out. Use Forgot Password? on the sign-in page — that works and sets your password.
This account has not been opened yet. Your sign-in exists but has not been switched on. It tells you to ask an administrator, which is who can do it.
This account has been deactivated. Access was switched off; your classes and history are kept. The office can reactivate you.
The school has sent too many sign-up emails… The school's hourly email allowance is spent, often because several people were invited at once. Wait an hour.
A reinvite link that dead-ends Reinvite sends a password reset link rather than a fresh invitation, and sending a new one kills the old one. Use the most recent email.

Part 2 · Teaching

The daily loop: a class, work set in it, a register taken, and marks entered.

Building a class

➕ Create New Class, from your home screen or the Classes tab. The form is longer than it looks because two of its answers are awkward to change later.

FieldWorth knowing
Class Name *What everyone will call it.
Grading WeightThree choices: Even (50/50), Skill-Heavy (60/40) or Participation-Heavy (40/60). This decides how Academic and Participation combine into the quarter grade, and it is shown to pupils and parents on the class page. Pick it deliberately.
Subject *Grouped by Drive group — the hint under it tells you which group the subject belongs to, which is where work gets filed.
Grade RangeDefaults to All Grades.
Maximum StudentsDefault 30.
Room / FacilityOptional.
Secondary TeacherA co-teacher can set work, grade, write notes and take the register — but cannot edit, close or delete the class.
📅 Class ScheduleDay and period, one row per meeting. Add these. Without them the class shows "no time set" and it will not appear on anyone's register for the day.
A new class starts with no grade categories. Until you add some in 📊 Manage Grades → ⚙️ Manage Categories, the category dropdown on a new assignment reads "No categories". And when you add your first one it arrives at weight 0, which will not save — the total has to come to exactly 100%, so type 100 into it.

Closing a class at the end of the year is 🔒 Close Class. Pupils and parents keep their grades and history; nothing new can be added.

Reopening brings back the register as it was when you closed it. Anyone you had withdrawn stays withdrawn — the confirmation says so, and afterwards you are told how many were left off, so putting one back is a decision rather than something you discover three weeks later.

Setting work

Open the class → 📝 Assignments. Everything to do with grading lives on that screen too, deliberately: 📋 Gradebook, 📊 Manage Grades and ➕ Create New Assignment are along the top.

The four types, chosen first because they change the rest of the form:

📝 Regular Assignment

The normal one. Points, a due date, a category, handed in through the portal.

📢 Information Only

An announcement or a resource. Nothing to submit, nothing to grade, and it ignores the quarter filter so it stays visible.

🎮 Game Practice

Tied to one of the learning games, with a minimum score and play time, and RTC tiers for 90 / 80 / 70 per cent.

🎯 Skill Mastery

Tied to one skill in a subject tree, with a target mastery score.

Then the fields that catch people out:

  • Publish immediately is ticked by default. Untick it to draft.
  • Graded Offline / In-Person — for tests, presentations and anything done in the room. Pupils see "Offline Assignment" and are not asked to upload anything; you enter the mark yourself.
  • Assign to specific students only — everyone is pre-ticked, so untick rather than tick. Only the pupils you leave ticked will see it.
  • Due Date must fall inside the quarter, and the form will tell you the window and refuse dates outside it.
An assignment has no quarter of its own — its due date decides. The quarter dropdown on the form is only used to check the date. So moving a due date past a quarter boundary later moves the assignment into the next quarter, and Edit Assignment does not check the date the way Create does. If work disappears from the list, look at its due date first.

A second thing about editing: after Save Changes the list behind the modal does not redraw. You will see "Assignment updated successfully!" over stale values — close the Assignments screen and reopen it to see the change.

Taking the register

There are two registers and they do different jobs.

The daily register

📋 Take Daily Attendance on your home screen. Everyone scheduled that weekday, with five statuses:

P Present · A Absent · L Late · E Left Early · and Late & Left Early.

  • Choosing Late or Left Early asks which period they arrived or left.
  • Choosing Absent reveals an Excused tick and a note field.
  • Every change saves by itself, immediately. The row tells you — Saving…, then Saved. The Save Attendance button is only a retry for rows that did not go through; with nothing pending it says "Everything is already saved."
  • It refreshes every twenty seconds, so two people can take it at once without overwriting each other.
Absences a parent has already reported are filled in for you. They arrive with an orange 📅 planned absence caption. It only fills gaps — a pupil you have already marked present stays present.

The class register

Open the class → 📋 Class Attendance. Two buttons per pupil, ✅ and ❌, with Late / Left Early / both behind ⋯ Other. Tapping the mark a pupil already has clears it.

Under each name is a grey chip showing what the day says — Day: ⏰ Late · arrived P3, left P6. It is information, not a button.

📋 Match Master copies the day's register onto this lesson, translated to the period on screen: before they arrived is absent, the period they arrived is late, in between is present, the period they left is left-early, after is absent. A pupil with nothing recorded on the day is left alone rather than defaulted to present. Press Save Attendance afterwards to keep it.

Open the daily register first if a family has reported an absence. Planned absences are applied when the daily register opens, not the class one. Go straight to a class register and Match Master will tell you the day has nothing recorded, even though a parent told the school days ago.

🚫 Cancel Class marks the lesson cancelled for that date, with an optional reason, and removes the attendance for that session. ↩️ Restore Class undoes it.

Marking

Three ways, for three different situations.

📋 Gradebook — everything at once

Every pupil down the side, every assignment across the top, one box each. Type, tab, type. Changed boxes get a coloured border and the footer counts ⚠️ N unsaved changes until you press Save Changes.

Example only — invented names and marks.

  • An excused cell shows EX and has no box. You cannot un-excuse from here — that is 📋 View Submissions → ✏️ Edit Status.
  • Draft assignments have no column. The Gradebook only shows published work, so an unpublished assignment can only be marked one pupil at a time.
  • Clicking a pupil's Academic cell opens the breakdown — trend 60%, recent five 40%, and the spread of A–F.

📝 One submission at a time

📋 View Submissions on an assignment. Filter by All / Submitted / Not Submitted / Graded / Excused, then 📝 Grade a pupil. You get their work, a rubric or a points box, and a Feedback field — which is the bit pupils actually read.

On a pupil who handed in nothing you also get ✋ Excuse and 📧 Remind.

📊 Manage Grades — the quarter grade

One row per pupil: Academic Avg, Participation, Suggested, Final Grade and Notes. Academic is calculated but you can override it, Participation is yours to set, and Use This accepts the suggestion as the final grade. ↺ system puts an overridden Academic back to the calculated one.

Do not open ⚙️ Manage Categories with unsaved grade edits on screen. It replaces the screen and your pending edits go with it, without a warning. Switching quarter does warn you; this does not. Save first.

My Students

Everyone in your classes, in one place, with two tabs: Directory and Notes.

Open a pupil and you get Overview (contact details and their strike history), Contacts, Medical, RTC, Notes, Skill Trees, Weekly Activity and Activity Log — plus their strike count and RTC balance in the header.

Three kinds of note, and only one is private

Written fromCalledWho can read it
Class roster → 📝 RecordStudent noteOther staff, and the pupil's parents. It is a permanent record entry.
Class roster → 🔒 My NotesPrivate noteOnly you.
Class Actions → 🔒 Class NotesClass noteOnly you. About the lesson, not a pupil.
A "📝 Record" note is parent-visible. Parents read these on the class screen under Children's Notes, with your name on them. There is no privacy flag on that form. If it is a thought rather than a record, use 🔒 My Notes.

A record note takes a sentiment (positive, negative, neutral), a category, an optional point value, and the note itself. The value has to agree with the sentiment — positive points for a positive note.

Riven, the assistant

The circle in the bottom-right corner of the portal. It is for teachers and admins only, which is why pupils and parents never mention it. Press it and ask for things in plain English.

rivertech.me/portal/ — Riven
Riven build 2026-09-18·a
Your AI Teaching Assistant
👥 Students 📋 Briefing ⌘K Search ❓ Help ✨ Smarter 🧹 Clear
Ask Riven anything…

The build number in the header is how you tell whether you have the latest version.

Things it genuinely understands, in roughly these words:

  • "Give Rosalind 50 RTC for great work" · "+5 gold to Ari"
  • "Who was absent yesterday?" · "What's Milo's attendance in maths this month?"
  • "Note for Ari: forgot homework again" · "Any notes mentioning homework this week?"
  • "How is my Robotics class doing?" · "Which students are failing?"
  • "Mark Junie present in maths" · "Cancel all my classes today except P1"
  • "Assign chapter 4 problems to Robotics due Friday"
  • "Book the gym Friday 2pm to 3pm" — it checks for clashes and files it for approval
  • "Report card for Ada" · "Anything I should know?" · "Undo that"

Press ❓ Help for the full list, 📋 Briefing for what needs attention, and ⌘K to search pupils and commands. Typing a / gives you the slash commands if you prefer them.

It asks before it changes anything, and it will not do an admin's job. When a request is admin-only it says so plainly — "Only an admin can … Ask the office — you can still look it up here any time." — rather than walking you through a confirmation that was going to be refused.

✨ Smarter offers a one-off ~1 GB download that runs a language model on your own device — nothing is sent anywhere — and makes Riven better at unclear phrasing and conversation. It is optional, it can be toggled off, and on a browser without WebGPU it will be slow. Chrome handles it best.

Everything else

💬 Messages

Your To: list holds the pupils in your own classes and every member of staff. Parents are not on it. To reach a family, open the pupil's record for their contact details.

🏢 Book a Room

From your home screen. Rooms are labelled Requires approval or Auto-approved, and My Bookings shows yours with a status you can cancel from. An administrator approves the ones that need it.

📦 Request Materials

A title, a category (supplies, technology, furniture, books, other) and a priority. It files as pending; the office works the queue.

📝 Testing

Build a test from multiple-choice and write-in questions, then Assign it to one or more classes with an optional due date. Multiple-choice marks itself; anything with a written answer waits for you. Results can be pushed into the gradebook.

The time limit is not enforced, and nothing is proctored. The minutes you set are displayed on the card and nothing more — there is no countdown and no auto-submit, and there is no fullscreen lock, tab-switch detection or question shuffling. Treat a Testing Centre test as open-book unless you are in the room.

🏆 Activities and 🧭 Staff Duties

If you coach, 🏆 Activities shows your teams with a Roster and Manage button — move a pupil from tryout to active with the status dropdown. There is no per-activity register; attendance in this portal is class-based.

🧭 Staff Duties opens on My duties — your week, by day, with who else is on with you. Everyone shows the whole rota with your own name in bold. An administrator sets them up.

🏃 PE Assessment and 🤖 Tech Projects

Two separate pages, each with its own class picker, for the practical work a terminal cannot mark. What you record there is verified evidence and feeds straight into the pupil's skill tree — a fitness result at or above "meets" masters the skill in their Training Log.

💰 RTC

Award it per pupil from their record, or to a whole class at once with 💰 Award RTC in Class Actions — set an amount, give a reason, submit. You can also log a real-world purchase and grant or revoke a privilege. Editing the catalogue itself is an administrator's job.

Save Preferences

The boxes show what you will actually receive, including before you have ever saved anything — so opening this card and pressing Save Preferences without touching a box changes nothing.

Strikes and attendance alerts are opt-in for teachers. Administrators get attendance alerts by default; you do not, because you would otherwise be emailed about every pupil in the school arriving late rather than just yours. Tick them if you want them.

You always get an in-app notice on the 🔔 bell when one of your pupils is given a strike, whether or not you want the email.

Google Drive

Where pupils' uploaded work is filed. It is not on the Profile screen — it is 📁 Setup Google Drive in Quick Actions on your portal home.

Check whether the school Drive is already connected before you connect your own. If it is, that screen says so: work is filed under Group › Subject › Class › Assignment in the school's Drive, you do not need to connect a personal one, and connecting one will not be used while the school Drive is set up. Only an administrator can connect the school Drive.

If you do connect your own, the portal creates a folder called RiverTech Student Assignments and files submissions into it by class and assignment. 🔌 Disconnect Drive stops new uploads going there — it does not withdraw the permission you gave Google, so if reconnecting ever misbehaves, revoke the app in your own Google account settings first and then connect again.

How the site looks

🎨 Site Theme on the Profile screen, and it follows your account to any device you sign in on. Six presets — Default Dark, Midnight Blue, Light, Warm Ember, Nord and AMOLED Black — then three optional extras folded away underneath: individual colours, a background image, and a button colour override.

There is no text-size setting; use your browser's zoom (Ctrl and +).

Signing out, and the account actions

⚙️ Account Actions on the Profile screen holds four buttons: 🏠 Back to Main Portal, 🚪 Logout, 🗑️ Delete Account and 🐛 Report a Bug.

You stay signed in on a browser until you press Logout — there is no timeout — so sign out on anything shared, including a classroom machine.

Delete Account deletes the classes you teach. It says so in the list it shows you, and it cannot be undone. If you are leaving, the right action is for an administrator to Deactivate you instead: you stop being able to sign in, and every class, grade and record you created stays where it is.

🐛 Report a Bug is worth using rather than mentioning it in passing. It asks for a category, a summary, what happened, the steps to reproduce it, and your browser — which is most of what anybody needs to fix it.
